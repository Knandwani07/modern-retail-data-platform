## Purpose

This visualization compares total revenue generated across product categories.

## Business Value

Understanding category-level revenue helps identify which product segments contribute the most to overall business performance and where future investments should be focused.

## Key Insights

- Toys generated the highest revenue.
- Electronics ranked as the second-highest revenue contributor.
- Home & Kitchen and Books generated moderate revenue.
- Sportswear produced the lowest revenue among all categories.

## Result

The analysis highlights that Toys and Electronics are the primary revenue-driving categories and contribute a significant portion of overall sales performance.
