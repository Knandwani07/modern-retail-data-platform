# 📦 Order Distribution by Product Category

<img width="1456" height="654" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/35db63f5-8b02-4815-8ece-f1d0d93c1242" />

## Purpose

This visualization shows the number of orders recorded across each product category.

## Business Value

Order volume analysis helps understand customer purchasing behavior and demand patterns across different categories.

## Key Insights

- Toys received the highest number of orders.
- Electronics ranked second in total order volume.
- Home & Kitchen maintained moderate demand.
- Books received fewer orders compared to leading categories.
- Sportswear recorded the lowest number of orders.

## Result

The analysis reveals customer demand distribution across categories and highlights which product segments attract the highest purchasing activity.
