## Purpose

This visualization ranks products based on total revenue generated from customer purchases.

## Business Value

Product-level revenue analysis helps identify top-performing items, optimize inventory planning, and prioritize products for marketing campaigns and promotions.

## Key Insights

- A small group of products generates a significant share of total revenue.
- The highest-performing products consistently outperform the rest of the catalog.
- Several products contribute minimal revenue and may require further evaluation.

## Result

The analysis identifies the products that drive the largest share of revenue, providing valuable insights for sales strategy, inventory management, and business growth planning.
